A coin is born in the light and crosses into the dark

Every coin here lives twice. It is born on pump.fun, where the crowd can see it, trade it and chase it. And whenever you choose, you burn it there and it is reborn on Monero, one for one, where no sender, no receiver and no amount survive. Monero has no token layer and no smart contracts. Neither does Bitcoin. The Monero side is what Ordinals did on BTC: write the thing into the transaction itself and let a reader find it.

One summoning makes a pair. A pump.fun token on Solana and its twin inscribed into a Monero transaction: same name, same ticker, same billion supply, locked 1:1. You trade on pump.fun's curve with everyone else, in SOL, and at 85 SOL it ascends to PumpSwap like any pump.fun coin. Nothing is minted on Monero that was not burned on Solana.

Every coin is created from the launchpad's own wallet, so pump.fun's creator share on every trade comes home. The launchpad keeps 3% of every trade made here and 0.7 SOL at birth. That pays for the inscriptions, the reader, and the hands that carry your bag across until the reader runs on its own.
